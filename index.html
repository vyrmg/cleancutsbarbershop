The shop

Waukegan's neighborhood barbershop

Clean Cuts Eye Candy Barbershop sits at 2208 Grand Avenue, near the intersection of Grand Ave and Berwick Blvd. Step inside and you'll find a classic barbershop atmosphere with music, friendly conversation, and skilled barbers who take pride in every cut.

Whether you need a sharp fade, a meticulous beard trim, or your child's first haircut, the team delivers personalized grooming at prices that keep customers coming back.
